Ask five Austin pool companies what weekly service costs and you'll get five different
numbers — not because anyone's ripping you off, but because "weekly service" means
different things to different companies. Some include chemicals. Some don't. Some visit
four times a month, some three. So before you can compare prices, you have to compare the
*same job*.

Here's the scope we price against, and what it costs in Austin right now.

## The standard scope

For a typical residential pool, "full weekly service" should mean:

- A weekly visit: skim, brush, and vacuum as needed
- Water testing and chemical balancing
- Basket and filter checks, with a backwash when it's due
- **Chemicals included** in the monthly price

Against that scope, the fair local range in Austin is **$140–$220 a month**. If a quote
comes in well below that, it usually means chemicals are billed separately or the pool is
visited less often — so read the fine print before you assume it's a better deal.

## What moves the price

Three things explain most of the spread:

1. **Chemicals in or out.** This is the biggest one. A "$110/month" quote with chemicals billed on top can easily land higher than a "$180 all-in" quote once summer chlorine demand kicks in.
2. **Summer vs. year-round.** Some companies charge a flat rate all year; others add a small summer premium because Texas heat means more chemicals and more attention. Ask which you're getting so July doesn't surprise you.
3. **Pool size and features.** Bigger pools, spas, and water features take more time and product.

## The one question that saves the most money

Ask: **"Is this price all-in with chemicals, and is it flat year-round?"** That single
question makes two quotes actually comparable — and it's where most of the confusion
(and overpaying) hides.
